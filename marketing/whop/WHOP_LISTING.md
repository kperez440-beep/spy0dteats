## 6. FAQ (replace all current questions)

**What exactly do I get?**
Live SPY alerts, the robot's reasoning, daily positions, a premarket brief and a recap, all in Discord.

**How does it help my trading?**
It flags setups as they form, grades them Strong / Decent / Weak, and explains why. That helps you time entries, skip weak setups, and make informed decisions instead of trading on impulse.

**Are these alerts guaranteed to be profitable?**
No. No alert service can guarantee profits, and we don't claim to. Alerts are information to help you make your own decisions. Options, especially 0DTE, carry substantial risk.

**Do you provide strike prices or trade setups?**
Alerts include the direction, a quality grade, the price zone the system is watching, an invalidation level, and a "don't chase" level. Daily positions show the contract the system took on its paper account. What you trade, and which strike, is always your decision.

**Does the system trade real money?**
Not yet. It paper trades (simulated) every session, and the positions we share are paper positions.

**Does it trade for me?**
No. You decide whether to place any trade in your own account.

**What experience do I need?**
You should already understand SPY options and 0DTE risk. This is not a beginner course.

**How do I cancel?**
Anytime, in one click through Whop.
